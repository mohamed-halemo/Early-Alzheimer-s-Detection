Hippocampus-focused VGG16 for CN / MCI / AD classification from T1 MRI

BrainAware takes a T1-weighted brain MRI, extracts the axial slices that contain the hippocampus, runs each slice through a fine-tuned VGG16 and averages the class probabilities. This page shows the viewer and the preprocessing on a few open-data scans.

Demo. Preprocessing approximated without FSL; open-data scans (healthy subjects), not ADNI. Research only, not for clinical use.

What the model sees

Each axial slice in the hippocampus range is skull stripped, cropped to the brain and resized to 150×150. The model classifies each slice on its own and averages the probabilities. Use the slider or the axial view to step through the slices.

About this demo

  • Scans are open IXI T1 MRI of healthy volunteers, not ADNI.
  • Preprocessing is approximated without FSL: affine registration to MNI152 (SimpleITK), MNI brain mask for skull stripping, Harvard-Oxford atlas for the hippocampus.
  • Static page: no uploads, no server, no tracking. The full app needs the Django backend.
  • Research only, not for clinical use.

How it works

  1. Data. ADNI T1-weighted MRI, 1,598 subjects (339 AD, 452 CN, 807 MCI).
  2. Preprocessing. Register to MNI152, segment the hippocampus, skull strip, remove background, keep the slices that contain the hippocampus, crop to the brain, resize to 150×150.
  3. Model. VGG16 pretrained on ImageNet. First 3 conv layers frozen, the rest fine-tuned, new head with Dense 1024 → 512 → 3 (softmax).
  4. Training. Adam, exponential LR decay (0.96), class weights for the imbalance, progressive augmentation (strength increased in steps during training).
  5. Inference. Predict each slice, average the probabilities over slices, report CN / MCI / AD percentages.
